# v0.24.0 Release Notes

**[⬇ Download obsidian-vault-compiler-v0.24.0.zip](obsidian-vault-compiler-v0.24.0.zip)**

**Released:** 2026-11-10

## New features

### An embed inside a sentence puts the note into the sentence (US-1.6, #107, #125)
An embed with other text on its line now puts the note's words into that
line, without heading and line breaks:

```markdown
He said ![[Sage]] and left.      →   He said Wise words. and left.
```

- Works in quotes, list items and headings, for whole notes and sections
  (`![[Sage#Detail]]`), with "Normalise headings" on and off.
- The note's text is kept exactly; its headings are left out; the lines of
  one paragraph are joined by a space.
- Only for a note that is one paragraph without its headings. Any other note
  is embedded as before, with the new warning *Note embedded in a sentence
  is not one paragraph; embedded as a block*.
- A note put into a sentence is not a part of the book of its own: links to
  it get *link target … not embedded*, unless it is also embedded on a line
  of its own.

### A caption for a map (US-4.11, #119, #126)
`# vault-compiler: caption <text>` in a map sets the picture's caption:
`![Branches of the fighting skills](assets/textmapper/….png)`. Text Mapper
ignores the line, so the map in Obsidian is unchanged. `\`, `[` and `]` are
escaped; a `|` (where Obsidian2Typst ends a caption) and a second caption
line are reported.

## Changed behaviour
- **Maps have no caption by default** (US-4.11, #119). Before, the picture
  took the label of the first labelled hex (`![Fog Lantern|Fog Lantern ](…)`),
  `<file> Map` from an `include` line, or `TextMapper Map`, which
  Obsidian2Typst printed as the map's caption. Now `![](…)`. In the fixture
  books only these map lines changed.
- **Every warning names a note and a line** (US-8.4 AC-6, #56). Map warnings
  (renderer not available, render failed, unknown directive) name the line
  of the map's opening fence instead of `compiled-output` / line 0;
  *Heading depth clamped* names the clamped heading, or the embed's line for
  a title, a prefix depth or a section; `<a href>` warnings name their line
  in the note, not in the merged document.

## Bug fixes
- **A note that is only one embed of a section was dropped** (US-6.5 AC-11,
  AC-12, #123, #124). With "Normalise headings" on (and in quotes always),
  `![x](../Rules/How%20it%20goes.md#Duel%20Checks)` as a note's only content
  looked up the heading still percent-encoded, left the section out and
  reported *Missing heading anchor*. Such wrapper notes are now read like
  every embed: escapes decoded, relative paths, `<…>`, paths without `.md`.
  A missing heading in such a note now leaves the hidden warning in the
  document and reports the embed's line (was line 0).

## Breaking changes and migration
- Maps lose their automatic captions. To keep one, add a
  `# vault-compiler: caption …` line to that map.
